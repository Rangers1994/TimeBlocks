TimeBlocks Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

TimeBlocks is a day-planning app that respects your privacy completely. The short version: your data never leaves your device, and we collect nothing.

Data Collection

TimeBlocks does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data Storage

Everything you create in TimeBlocks — your day schedules, activity categories, and templates — is stored locally on your device only. The app makes no network connections. Your data is included in your normal device backups (iCloud or computer backups) under your control, governed by Apple's backup privacy practices.

Third Parties

TimeBlocks contains no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, or advertising networks. No data is shared with anyone because no data is collected.

Children's Privacy

TimeBlocks is suitable for all ages, including students. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, no personal information is ever gathered from children or anyone else.

Deleting Your Data

All app data lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
