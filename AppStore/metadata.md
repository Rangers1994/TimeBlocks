## Description (4000 chars max)

TimeBlocks makes time management visible. Your day becomes a grid of 96 blocks — one for every 15 minutes — and you simply paint it with color-coded, emoji-labeled activities. Sleep, school, homework, sports, free time: see your whole day at a glance, plan it in under two minutes, and find out at the end of the month where your time really went.

Built for students learning to manage their time — and for anyone who wants an honest picture of their day.

PAINT YOUR DAY
• A full day on one screen: 24 hours × four 15-minute blocks
• Drag across the grid to fill blocks with an activity
• Color-coded categories with emoji labels make the day instantly readable

YOUR CATEGORIES, YOUR COLORS
• Start with built-in categories like Sleep, School, Homework, Sports, and Free Time
• Create your own with any name, color, and emoji
• Reorder the palette to keep your most-used activities on top

PLAN FASTER WITH TEMPLATES
• Save any planned day as a weekday or weekend template
• Apply a template to other days with a simple drag and drop on the calendar
• New days start sensibly pre-filled: sleep at night, free time elsewhere

HONEST MONTHLY SUMMARIES
• See total hours per activity for any month
• Spot trends: is homework time growing? Is sleep shrinking?
• Review up to 12 months of history

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your device — no account, no sign-up, no tracking
• No ads, no analytics, nothing collected
• An entire year of schedules uses less than a megabyte

Whether you are a student building time-management habits, a parent teaching them, or anyone curious where the hours go — TimeBlocks turns an abstract 24 hours into something you can see, touch, and improve.
