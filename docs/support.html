About

TimeBlocks makes time management visible. Your day becomes a grid of 96 blocks — one for every 15 minutes — and you paint it with color-coded, emoji-labeled activities. Built for students learning to manage their time, and for anyone who wants an honest picture of their day.

How to Use

  • Plan a day: Tap any day on the calendar, pick an activity from the palette, and drag across the grid to paint blocks.
  • Categories: Use the built-in activities or create your own with any name, color, and emoji.
  • Templates: Long-press a planned day to save it as a weekday or weekend template, then drag the template onto other days to apply it instantly.
  • Summaries: Open the summary tab to see total hours per activity for any month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the app free?

Yes, TimeBlocks is completely free.

Does the app need internet?

No. TimeBlocks works entirely offline. All of your schedules stay on your device.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, sign-ups, or logins.

How far back can I review my time?

You can view and edit up to 12 months of history, and plan up to 1 month ahead.
