TimeBlocks - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Overview

TimeBlocks ("the App") is a day-planning app that makes time management visible. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The App:

  • Does not require an account or login
  • Does not collect your name, email, or any personal information
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising
  • Does not connect to the internet
  • Does not share any data with third parties

How the App Works

TimeBlocks runs entirely on your device. Your day schedules, activity categories, and templates are stored locally and never leave your device. Your data is included in your normal device backups (iCloud or computer backups) under your control, governed by Apple's backup privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages, including students. It does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Deleting Your Data

All app data lives on your device. Deleting the App deletes all of its data.
